### Articles
- Robert J. Dolan (2014), Marketing Reading: Framework for Marketing Strategy Formation, Online product on Harvard Business Press https://hbsp.harvard.edu/import/1445951 you have to order the PDF online for $8.85, see Canvas. P1 – p13 are mandatory for this course.
- The external analysis models should be familiar to you. In this course we only focus on the Consumer Analysis part, all other parts are assumed to be known. Well-known models that can help you to do external analyses (cf. 4 of the 5 C’s in the Dolan 2014 model) can be structured according to the level of analysis, ie, the Macro, Meso, and the Micro environment:
	- Macro environment analysis: (DE)STEP, PEST(EL) analysis models. See eg, the marketing theories links on the site of: https://www.professionalacademy.com/professional-qualifications/cim-marketing-qualifications/marketing-theories/
	- Meso (industry) environment analysis: Porter (2008), The five competitive forces that shape strategy, Harvard Business Review ,86(1), p78
	- Microenvironment analysis: any competitor (group) analyses, see eg. also the Professional Academy site for the most common analytical competitor models. And all consumer (target) group analyses, which will be discuss during the consumer behavior sessions in this course.
	- The fifth C, the Company analysis, represents the internal analysis models which will be discussed in week 2.
