
# What is Marketing
EVolution of the marketing concept
1. The production concept
	1. Company will buy products that are widely available and low in cost
2. The product Concept
	1. Consumers will buy the products that offer the best quality or performance
3. The selling concept
	1. Organization must undertake aggressive selling efforts to make customers buy
4. The Marketing Concept
	1. not only pushing what the business wants but listening to the consumers and framing the products to their needs

Marketing Definitions (AMA)
1985: process of planning and executing conception, pricing, promotion and distribution of goods, ideas and services to create exchanges that satisfy individual and organizational goals
*transaction and product driven*

2012: Marketing is the activity, set of institutions , and processes for creating communicating, delivering and exchanging offerings that have value for customers clients, partners, and society at large.
*relation and value driven*
- how much can you "exprimir" from the customers you already have = customer retention
- Looking at what they're purchasing and why -> turning that into value

Market Orientation as organization behavior: ability to generate, disseminarte and use superior information about customers and competitors
