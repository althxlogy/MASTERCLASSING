## Executive Summary

The modern landscape of competitive advantage has shifted from operational excellence—such as lean manufacturing and superior distribution—to a dedicated focus on customer centricity. Today, the primary driver of growth is a firm’s "insights engine": an innovative organizational capability that transforms disparate data into deep understandings of consumer motivations and translates those insights into strategy.

Evidence from the global **Insights2020 (i2020)** study, which surveyed over 10,000 practitioners, reveals that a firm's insights and analytics (I&A) function is the single most important factor in driving customer-centric growth. High-performing organizations ("overperformers") are characterized by insights functions that are independent, strategically integrated, and balanced between analytical and creative thinking. Unilever serves as the primary case study for this transformation, having evolved its market research department from a reactive service unit into a strategic "Consumer and Market Insights" (CMI) group with the mission to "inspire and provoke to enable transformational action."

## The Strategic Shift: From Data to Action

In the past, market research was a reactive function that provided performance reports. In the current era, leading firms have elevated this function into an "insights engine." The transition involves moving beyond merely amassing data to connecting dots and extracting strategic value.

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
|Capability|Traditional Market Research|Modern Insights Engine|
|**Role**|Reactive service unit|High-level strategic advisor|
|**Focus**|Performance reporting and data supply|Distilling motivations and driving strategy|
|**Goal**|Providing insights|Enabling transformational action|
|**Reporting**|Reports to marketing function|Reports to C-suite/Executive Board|

## Seven Operational Characteristics of the Insights Engine

The i2020 research identifies seven operational traits that distinguish superior insights functions.

### 1. Data Synthesis

Overperforming firms are nearly twice as likely as underperformers (67% vs. 34%) to be skilled at linking disparate data sources.

- **One Version of the Truth:** Unilever uses a global marketing-information system to ensure all departments (marketing, finance, etc.) view the same data in the same format.
- **Proprietary Tools:**
    - **People Data Centre:** Combines social media, business analytics, and data mining of customer-care lines.
    - **PeopleWorld:** An AI platform that allows employees to mine 70,000 research documents using natural-language queries.

### 2. Independence

Superior insights groups sit outside traditional functions like marketing.

- **C-Suite Access:** Leaders in overperforming organizations report to senior executives (CEO, CSO) more than twice as often as those in underperforming firms (29% vs. 12%).
- **Unilever Structure:** The head of CMI reports directly to the executive board, allowing the function to challenge organizational projects objectively.

### 3. Integrated Planning

Insights leaders are included in all key stages of the business- and brand-planning cycle.

- **"Where to Play":** Using tools like **Growth Scout** to quantify the value of category or brand penetration across demographics and regions.
- **"How to Win":** Using **Growth Cockpit** to provide a one-screen overview of brand performance against competitors to identify growth opportunities.

### 4. Collaboration

69% of respondents from overperforming firms work closely with other functions and customers, compared to 52% of underperformers.

- **Cross-Functional Colocation:** CMI team leaders are colocated with presidents of product categories (e.g., personal care, home care).
- **Shared Responsibility:** CMI is held accountable for business results, creating an incentive to partner deeply with commercial teams.

### 5. Experimentation

Overperforming companies are three times more likely (40% vs. 13%) to embrace a culture of experimentation.

- **The Foundry:** An incubator for marketing-technology start-ups and a platform for hackathons.
- **Shark Tank:** An initiative where start-ups pitch new technologies to CMI executives; the program has piloted over 175 technologies and scaled 37.

### 6. Forward-Looking Orientation

Sophisticated practitioners use predictive analytics to both anticipate and influence future behavior.

- **Trend Shaping:** Unilever’s "All Things Hair" YouTube channel analyzes a billion Google searches a month to identify hairstyle trends and create how-to videos, driving purchases three times more effectively than conventional ads.
- **Human and Cultural Futures (HCF):** A dedicated team that examines macro forces (societal, technological, environmental) to imagine the future of consumer needs.

### 7. Affinity for Action

79% of insights functions at overperforming companies participate in strategic decision-making at all levels.

- **Strategy over Data:** CMI focuses on specific recommendations, such as promoting nighttime toothbrushing to bond fathers and children, which simultaneously drives usage and supports Unilever's social mission.

## Three People Characteristics of the Insights Engine

Success depends not only on operations but on the mindset and talent of the people within the function.

### 1. Whole-Brain Mindset

While insights functions were historically "left-brain" (analytical), superior engines integrate "right-brain" (creative) approaches.

- **Training:** Unilever uses "Upping Your Elvis" workshops to push employees toward creative problem-solving.
- **Direct Connection:** Through the **People Voice** program, 30,000 employees annually—from factory workers to the CEO—connect directly with customers to gain unmediated insights.

### 2. Business Focus

75% of overperformers believe their insights functions are business-focused, compared to 50% of underperformers.

- **Financial Alignment:** CMI staff bonuses are linked to wider business unit performance to ensure shared accountability.
- **Capability Building:** The "CMI Academy" offers courses like "finance for nonfinance managers" to build business acumen.

### 3. Storytelling

Overperformers are significantly more skilled at conveying messages through narratives (61% vs. 37%).

- **"Show, Don't Tell":** CMI has moved away from charts and tables in favor of provocative, TED-style talks.
- **Experiential Learning:** To build empathy for senior citizens, Unilever marketing executives wore old-age simulation gear while trying to use products, leading to the redesign of ice cream packaging for better readability.

## Conclusion

The insights engine is the most significant driver of customer-centric growth identified in the i2020 research. However, for an insights engine to be effective, it requires top-down leadership to ensure every department maintains a singular focus on meeting consumer needs. As demonstrated by Unilever’s CMI, the ultimate goal of an insights engine is not to produce data, but to inspire the transformational actions that lead to business growth.
