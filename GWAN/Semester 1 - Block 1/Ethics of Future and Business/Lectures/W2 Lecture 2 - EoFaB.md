## Circular Economy
- most products nowadays are built with planned obsolescence
- circularity requires materials to be used for as long as possible
- ideally in circularity trash does NOT exist, it is completely eliminated as "waste" becomes re-used
- principles of circles:
	- design out waste and pollution
	- keep products and materials in use
	- Regenerate natural systems
- 3 circular strategies
	- REtaining product ownership
		- circularity as a service - libraries BEEN doing this
	- Product Life Extension
		- Long-lasting products
	- Design for recycling
		- Eco-efficiency and innovation

The strategy is contingent on: how easily you can get the product back? & how easily can value be recovered from it?
