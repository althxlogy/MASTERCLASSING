### Ethical Egoism
- usually not considered as an ethical teoru but is influential of economics
- an action is morally right if the decision maker freely decides in order to pursue either their (short term) desires or their (long term ) interests
- The influence of egoism is related to the perception of ideal markets and "Invisible Hand"(by Adam Smith)
- Problems
	- Inconsistent since criminals' interests are also taken into account
	- moral view prioritizes short term satisfaction of needs and contradicts moral principles
	- enlightened egoism focuses on accomlpishing long term individual interests
- Application to the self-driving car: car would protect the people in the car


### Utilitarianism
greatest amount of good for the greatest amount of people
influential in normative economics -> related to the egoistic view of humans in economics

Egoism in economics tends to function wtihin a utilitarian theory

act utilitarianism: single action of maximizing common good
Rule Utilitarianism: developing a rule that maximizes common good throughout multiple situations

Problems: 
- common good is subjective
- can contradict basic rights and  runs into difficulties with the distribution of wealth
- Lack of idea of reciprocity

What the car doing: conduct a cost/benefit analysis and maximize good

### Ethics of duties
Universal applicability: check that the principle is acceptable to every human being
Respect for persons: A human being is a vulnerable autonomically being and not a means to an end or a tool

Problem: difficult and abstract theory
- undervalues outcomes of actions
- Morality can be seen as a heavy burden

What da car doing: car would sacrifice itself to not involve people that have nothing to do with the situation. Person who bought the car is at"fault" because it was their decision to make the purchase

### Rights and justice
Natural (Human) Rights: basic and important entitlements that should be respected and protected in every single situatino - based in dignity and subsequently carry a duty to protect and respect others
examples: freedom og speech, property, life, freedom, privacy, etc

Justice: simultaneous fair treatment of individuals in a given situation with the result that everybody gets what they deserve

Problems:
- rights can be seen as arbitrary
- Can be criticized for being too Western and neocolonial
- Basic rights presuppose institutions when not all places have the sufficient resources or well-structure institutions to enact that justice properly
