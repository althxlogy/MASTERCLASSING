# Course Structure and themes

Ethics = systematic study of right and wrong (at the individual level in this course)
Responsibility = tripple bottom line in a company (Organizational Level)
Sustainability = Human Welfare within ecological balance (societal level)

Moratlity is  humans ability to distinguish between right and wrong
Ethical theories are principles and rules that determine right and wrong in different situations

Normative ethics is the study of "how do I behave"

# Ethics
- on-going discussion about morality with a very long history
- aim is to take theories into practice
- Different theories an give contradictory results
	- Important to question what are the principles/theories and how do they impact my actions

## Ethics vs Law
- Morality is the foundation of the law in our society
- Law does not cover all ethical issues, not all legal issues are ethical, and law and ethics can contradict each other
- Fine line between unethical and illegal
- Companies can operate in locations without legal infrastructure - taking advantage of unethical principles
	- Legislation might be available but not enforcement
