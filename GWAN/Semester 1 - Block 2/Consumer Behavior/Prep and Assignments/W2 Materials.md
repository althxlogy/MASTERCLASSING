- hot vs cool systems of thinking
	- Cool thinking systems are cognitive and include memory and elaborations. thought processes that are not highly effected by emotions | slower, more complex
	- Hot systems congnitions are relevant in high emotional contexts and are faster/reflexive
	- People can feel emotional significance for all types of stimulus
- Appraisals make us feel a range of different emotions. The following appraisals reflect how are emotions are formed (and in the direciton they are made)
	- motivational state:  reward vs punish
	- situational state: present vs absent
	- Probability: certain vs uncertain
	- Legitimacy: positive vs negative outcome deserved
	- causal agency: other person vs self

- What is affect and how does it influence a consumer's emotional response
	- affect influences consumer attitude regardless of cognitive structure (i assume regardless of whether it's hot or cold)
	- affect can also determine longer lasting consumer attitudes and change how they evaluate products
	- it influences whether the consumer's attention is being retained
	- also how emotionally engaged a consumer is to a brand
- affective responses are quick reactions to brand messaging
	- brands use emotional appeal to elicit emotional responses
- feelings are more effective conductors when the product matches their emotion better
	- the intensity of both is also important here. Low emotion marketing actions are better fits for people with a lower emotion threshold and vice versa
	- Seeing other people experience an emotion while using a product also affects us (think of influenceer reaction vids)
	- consumers under high time pressure are more likely to create an emotional connection rather than a cognitive connection
- There are many ways to quantify and measure emotions
	- e.g. PANAS: not at all to extremely (likert scale)
	- There are also ways to measure through biometric measures, EEG, coding facical expressions, etc. More objective and scientific approach
- Moods are different to emotions: moods are transient feeling states that are more simple descriptiors of how some one feels at a given moment while emotions are specific psychobiological reactions to appraisals
- Moods last longers and have a lower intensity than emotions
- Mood regulation: consumers tend to prefer products that allow them to keep a positive mood or help raise their mood if they are in a negative state. In a positive mood they also want to stay in the same level of arousal, but negative mood experiences choose the opposite level of arousal to what they feel
