## Main Ideas

photo and language are two pillars of advertising in a digital landscape. Social Media platforms like Instagram and Facebook have popularized advertising formats that consists of a photo with overlayed text. This article challenges common conceptions about how text is used relatively with photo to stimulate engagement from consumers in an online marketspace. The independent variable in the study is the text-image ratio (TiR), other tested moderating variables include text & visual entropy, sentiment, congruency, and social content. The study's dependent variable is social media engagement which is characterized by the # of likes and comments
