# Ratings
- Number vs text ratings: are mismatched often because of different personal/cultural understandings of what each rating stands for. For some people a 5/10 is neutral, while for others 5/10 is TERRIBLE
- Too good to be true effect: reviews are not reliable when too high  (at 5 stars or almost), it looks inorganic and often is 
	- It is also not hard to find inauthentic reviews: if a product has too many reviews too quickly then it is likely that they were fabricated by AI
- How do responses to reviews pay off: when a firm is responsive they have more frequent reviews. The length of the response has no impact on firm perception, but response time does. Therefore it doesn't matter much if a response isn't too long, the firm just has to respond quickly to give the impression that they are "on it"
