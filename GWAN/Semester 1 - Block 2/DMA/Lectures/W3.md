- Paralinguistics: Speech/communication that is not verbal and gives contextual info important to the message conveyed

- Emojis
	- Emojis are now an important way of communicating via paralinguistics
	- Consumer message appraisal and the outcomes of brand communications is dependent on emoji usage in today's marketing world - based on the following three variables:
		-  Emoji Symbolism:
			- Iconic:  represents the subject of the emoji and means almost exactly that/is directly related
			- Symbolic Emoji: represents a more abstract ideas and the emoji requires interpretation
		- Emoji syntax: they can be used to supplement text or can exist stand alone
		- Marketing Emoji Group relatedness: whether a consumer needs to be within the existing consumer group or not to understand the message - membership audience vs general audience
